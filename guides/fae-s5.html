1. Season 5 Lifecycle & Progression Roadmap

Fae Realm Season 5 is a 30-day continuous regional war divided into four distinct Settlement Phases. Alliances compete across opening map concentric rings to secure territorial nodes, build forward tower chains, and control the central Sacred Tree of Avalon.

Detailed Phase Breakdown & Timelines

Phase Stage Timeline Map Status & Gateway Unlocks Core Operational Focus
Phase 1: Arrival Days 1–5 Outer Ring Zones open. Gateways locked. Establish Alliance HQ, claim Level 1–2 Fae Mines, build base tower chains.
Phase 2: Frontier Days 6–12 Outer Gateways open into Middle Ring. Contest Level 1 Ports, Auxiliary Temples, Level 3 Mines. First Merchant Ships land.
Phase 3: Escalation Days 13–22 Inner Gateways open into Core Ring. Capture Major Temples (Dionysus/Aphrodite), secure Level 2–3 Ports, level up Fae Technology.
Phase 4: Climax Days 23–30 Sacred Tree Barrier drops. Central zone open. Rally Sacred Tree, secure Commander of the Realm title, finalize Military Rank grinding.

Scoring Engines: Settlement Points vs. Battlefield Points

Victory and ranking in Season 5 are calculated using two distinct scoring mechanisms. Understanding the operational difference between these two point engines is essential for alliance leadership:

  • Settlement Points (Passive Engine): Generated continuously every minute based on total linked alliance territory, occupied temples, active ports, and controlled mines. These points accumulate toward phase settlement rewards and dictate regional dominance rankings.
  • Battlefield Points (Active Engine): Earned directly through player actions: defeating enemy garrisons, destroying enemy rallies, conducting 3-0 Merchant Ship raids, and clearing Fae monsters. Battlefield points directly fuel individual Military Rank progression.
CRITICAL WAR MECHANIC: The Unbroken Tower Chain Rule All alliance towers MUST maintain an unbroken connected link back to the primary Alliance Fortress / Headquarters. If enemy forces sever a tower link, ALL disconnected towers downstream instantly enter a "Suppressed State"—ceasing Settlement Point accrual, disabling mine protection shields, and rendering attached temples vulnerable to immediate seizure!

Step-by-Step Day 1–30 Strategic Playbook

Step 1: Days 1–5 (Base Foundation)

Construct Alliance HQ in the outer safe zone. Direct all alliance members to build towers rapidly along high-density mine clusters. Do not waste alliance resources building toward locked gateways until Day 4.

Step 2: Days 6–12 (Frontier Breakthrough)

Anchor forward towers directly against Outer Gateways 3 hours prior to unlock. The moment gateways open, send primary rally teams to capture Auxiliary Temples (Apollo) to boost troop recovery speeds for upcoming battles.

Step 3: Days 13–22 (Temple Wars)

Launch synchronized coalition rallies against Major Temples (Dionysus and Aphrodite). Enforce strict 3-march defense walls instantly upon taking any structure to prevent instant enemy recapture.

Step 4: Days 23–30 (Sacred Tree & Final Settlement)

Focus full coalition power on holding the Sacred Tree. Simultaneously audit all alliance members to ensure everyone has reached the mandatory Bronze Knight 4 Stars threshold before the final settlement clock hits zero.

MAJOR PITFALL: "The Dead Branch Extension" Avoid building long, single-line tower chains ("snake towers") deep into enemy territory without building parallel support towers. A single enemy rally on an isolated tower can sever your entire forward network, stripping thousands of Settlement Points in a single fight.

Unlocking in Phase 4, the Sacred Tree of Avalon represents the supreme objective of Season 5. Holding the Sacred Tree continuously for 30 minutes earns the leading coalition leader the title of Commander of the Realm.

The Commander of the Realm gains exclusive access to the Realm Relic Vault, granting authority to distribute three powerful seasonal relics:

  • Relic of Supreme Might: Grants +15% Troop Attack and +10% Lethality to the assigned player.
  • Relic of Iron Fortitude: Grants +15% Troop Defense and +10% Damage Reduction to the assigned player.

WAR PITFALL: Unclaimed Relic Expiration Relics distributed by the Commander of the Realm must be claimed by recipient players within 24 hours via system mail. Failure to claim before the timer expires causes the relic to return to the vault, forfeiting its stat bonuses for the remainder of the settlement phase!

3. Rigid 3-March Defense Wall Protocol

Combat garrisons in Fae Season 5 operate under a unique engine rule: The 3-March Defense Wall. Unlike standard gameplay where all reinforcing troops merge into a single massive defense pool, Fae structures process combat sequentially through the first three marches present.

MANDATORY COMBAT RULE: Strict March Insertion Order The first three marches inside ANY temple or structure form the active frontline wall. Incoming enemy rallies hit ONLY these three marches. Marches 4 through 30 sit in a non-combat reinforcement queue behind the wall.

How the 3-March Defense Wall Works Mathematically

  • Active Frontline Wall (Slots 1, 2 & 3): Absorbs 100% of incoming damage. Combat stats, troop tier mix, damage reduction, and hero skills are calculated EXCLUSIVELY from these three specific marches.
  • Reserve Reinforcement Queue (Slots 4–30): Takes zero damage while queued. When one of the three frontline marches is completely wiped out or recalled, Slot 4 instantly steps forward to fill the vacant wall position.

Step-by-Step Frontline Defense Setup

Step 1: Declare Primary Defenders (Tanks)

Leadership assigns designated high-stat players (P1, P2, P3) as primary structure anchors. No other players are permitted to send marches until these tanks land.

Step 2: Execute Primary Landing

Designated primary tanks launch their best defensive marches (heavy Infantry/Cavalry mix with defensive gear) into the vacant structure.

Step 3: Verify Garrison Wall via Structure UI

Structure leader opens the Garrison Detail tab to verify that Slots 1, 2, and 3 belong strictly to the assigned primary defenders.

Step 4: Open Secondary Support Reinforcements

Once the wall is verified, general alliance members are authorized to send reinforcing marches to fill Queue Slots 4 through 30.

CATASTROPHIC WAR ERROR: "Scout / Weak March Intrusion" If a low-power player or a weak scout march lands inside a structure BEFORE the assigned primary tanks, that weak march occupies Slot 1, 2, or 3 on the frontline wall. An incoming enemy rally will instantly obliterate that weak march, resulting in massive loss of structure durability and a immediate coalition Morale drop!

Step-by-Step Protocol to Fix Wall Intrusion

  1. Garrison monitor identifies a weak/unassigned march in Slots 1–3.
  2. Immediately post in Alliance War Chat: "RECALL [Player Name] FROM [Structure Name] IMMEDIATELY!"
  3. The designated primary tank launches their march to land the exact moment the weak march exits.

Morale is the overarching combat balance engine in Season 5. Morale fluctuates dynamically based on structure ownership, victory streaks, and loss penalties, applying massive direct stat modifiers to all troops fighting across the map.

The Morale Snowball Effect

Controlling Morale Temples (Aphrodite) increases your rating. Conversely, losing consecutive battles drops your morale score. If your rating falls below 0, your troops enter a Morale Deficit, reducing damage output and making it harder to win subsequent battles.

Step-by-Step Morale Deficit Recovery Protocol

Step 1: Cease Direct Rallies on Major Temples

Do NOT launch rallies against enemy Major Temples (Dionysus) while suffering from a -10 or worse Morale Deficit. The stat penalty will cause your rallies to fail, worsening the deficit.

Step 2: Pivot Focus to Morale Temples (Aphrodite)

Redirect full alliance power to capture minor Auxiliary Morale Temples or unfortified ports to gain immediate +5 Morale points.

Step 3: Farm Merchant Ship Victories

Execute guaranteed 3-0 Crushing Victories against NPC Merchant Ships. Winning ship raids awards minor positive morale ticks, helping reset your rating back to Neutral (0).

Step 4: Resume Major Offensives

Once Morale reaches neutral (0) or positive territory, re-engage primary enemy Major Temples with full coalition rallies.

5. Merchant Ship Raids & Fae Shop Optimization

Merchant Ships spawn continuously from controlled Fae Harbors. Raiding ships is the primary method for acquiring individual Fae Crystals and grinding Military Experience points.

Merchant Ship Raiding Rules

When targeting Merchant Ships along coastal ports, players must adhere to strict hull inspection rules:

RAID RULE: Clean Hulls vs. Burning Hulls Always target Clean (Unlooted) Merchant Ships. Attacking a ship that is already burning or damaged by another player incurs a mandatory 50% PENALTY on Fae Crystal loot yield and cuts earned Military Experience in half!

3-0 Crushing Victory Execution Protocol

To maximize point yields from merchant raids, players should aim for a 3-0 Crushing Victory sweep against NPC ship guardians:

  1. Deploy three balanced marches featuring your strongest heroes.
  2. Ensure march speeds are synchronized using march speedups so all three marches land simultaneously.
  3. Defeating all three guardian waves without losing a single march awards the +25% Bonus Completion Chest containing rare Hero Crest fragments.
